Blockchains cannot readily read information about the real-world (weather, price, real-world-assets) into their domain of authority. For this, they need to trust an external source of truth, often called an Oracle.

> The oracle problem only focuses on reading information about the real world, but the same can be said about mutating/updating the real world as well.

Argument: For an authority to be [[Trustless]] , it needs to be able to readily read the state related to that matter, and readily (enforce or) write mutations to it, regardless of being in the digital or real world.
