authorities are entities that we place in society to **resolve conflicts** and **establish trust** between humans. These conflicts usually arise in *value-bearing* interactions, often as a result of scarcity. There is only so much gold in the world, therefore it bears value, and therefore when dealing with gold, we reside to needing an authority. See [[Need For Trust]]. 

Blockchain systems essentially can act as digital authorities, and can be modeled in any of the ways described in [[Blockchain Models]].
