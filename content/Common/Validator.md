A synonym often used in [[Blockchain Networks]] for those who author blocks ([[Blockchain Networks#Authoring Nodes]]) and those who _actively_ check the work of other authors.

Work done by the validators is often as secure [[Trustless]] as it can possibly be.

This term is most often used in [[Proof of Work and Proof of Stake#Proof of Stake]] networks, and in [[Proof of Work and Proof of Stake#Proof of Work]], they are called [[Miners]]
