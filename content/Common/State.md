The information and storage that the blockchain system holds. This term is derived from the [[State Machine]] model, one of the [[Blockchain Models]] of blockchains.

The state of the blockchain is the main storage primitive of blockchains. It is utmost secure and [[Trustless]] (meaning that we have access to its entire history as well), and calculating it essentially the outcome of the [[Consensus Algorithm]] of the blockchain.

It is not to be mistaken with [[The Bigger Picture#Storage]], which is a discussion around non-consensus storage primitives that can be be used the blockchain state for broader [[Web3]] applications.
