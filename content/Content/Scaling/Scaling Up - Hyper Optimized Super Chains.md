The first way to improve the scalability of a blockchain network is to not touch the architecture explained in [[Introduction - Why Scaling Matters]], but add a combination of hardware and software optimizations to combat this. [Solana](https://solana.com/pt/news/8-innovations-that-make-solana-the-first-web-scale-blockchain) is perhaps the best example of such an approach. This method evidently works as seen in high transaction rates of Solana, but also poses some challenges: 
- The [hardware requirements](https://docs.solanalabs.com/operations/requirements) to run a validator is increasingly high.
- End of the day, this method is a form of vertical scaling, and there are questions about whether it will grow indefinitely or not. 

All of the properties of vertical scaling discussed in [[Introduction - Why Scaling Matters#Vertical and Horizontal Scaling]] applies to this approach.
