The goals this part of the book is is to clearly understand *what*[^1] blockchains do, and *why* it is novel, and under _which circumstances_ it can be _useful_. While doing so, we will discover a bit _how_ they do it, but I will leave most of this to the readers to learn on their own. The _how_ part would inevitably be long, very technical, and frankly not a contributor to the [[Introduction#Ultimate Goal|ultimate goal]] of this book named before. 

We can see blockchain systems to some extent as a black-box machine. What matters is to understand *what* this machine can do, and therefore imagine what problems it can solve for us. It is less relevant to go to all the nitty gritty details of _how_ the machine works, if we are not yet sure what it can be used for.
